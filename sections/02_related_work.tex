\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Cooperative perception.} OPV2V provides a benchmark and fusion pipeline for vehicle-to-vehicle perception~\cite{opv2v}, while DAIR-V2X introduces real-world vehicle-infrastructure cooperative 3D detection~\cite{dairv2x}. V2VNet studies joint perception and prediction through exchanged vehicle representations~\cite{v2vnet}. HEAL addresses extensible heterogeneous collaboration~\cite{heal}, and Where2comm selects spatially informative communication content~\cite{where2comm}. DiscoNet~\cite{disconet}, When2com~\cite{when2com}, and HM-ViT~\cite{hmvit} explore distilled collaboration graphs, dynamic grouping, and heterogeneous multi-agent vision transformers. CoBEVT studies cooperative bird's-eye-view semantic segmentation from cameras~\cite{cobevt}. V2X-ViT~\cite{v2xvit} and F-Cooper~\cite{fcooper} introduce vision transformers and feature pooling for multi-agent fusion. Recent works also investigate relative pose errors in V2X systems~\cite{v2xreg,freealign}, attempting post-hoc geometric registration from predicted 3D bounding boxes. However, bounding-box registration remains bounded by monocular depth variance and degrades under severe noise. In contrast, \method{} infers multi-view geometry and object queries directly from surround-view images without external poses or brittle box-matching.

\textbf{Query-based 3D detection.} DETR introduced set prediction with object queries~\cite{detr}. For autonomous driving, multi-camera BEV representations often project 2D features via depth lifting, such as LSS~\cite{lss} and BEVFormer~\cite{bevformer}. DETR3D connects 3D queries to multi-view image features through projection~\cite{detr3d}, while PETR~\cite{petr} and PETRv2~\cite{petrv2} embed 3D position information into 2D features. Sparse4D~\cite{sparse4d} further demonstrates iterative sparse anchor refinement. Building on projected query sampling, our depth-guided query refinement mechanism integrates object-level surface grouping with iterative box-relative geometric encoding, providing explicit geometric feedback to evolving 3D hypotheses.

\textbf{Visual geometry and pseudo-point clouds.} Monocular and multi-view dense prediction has progressed rapidly through DPT~\cite{dpt} and Depth Anything~\cite{depthanything}. DUSt3R regresses geometric structure from multi-view images~\cite{dust3r}; VGGT predicts multiple geometric quantities using a shared transformer~\cite{vggt}. VGGT-$\Omega$ scales this architecture with inter-image token exchange~\cite{omega}, leveraging scalable self-supervised representations such as DINOv2~\cite{dinov2}. While one conventional route converts estimated depth into pseudo-point clouds~\cite{pseudolidar} for external point-cloud detectors like PointPillars~\cite{pointpillars} or CenterPoint~\cite{centerpoint}, \method{} connects visual geometry directly to 3D query detection, bypassing costly point-cloud export and separate detection stages.
