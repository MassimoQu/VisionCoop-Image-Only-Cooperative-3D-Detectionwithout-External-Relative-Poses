\section{Conclusion}
We presented \method{}, a camera-only-inference cooperative 3D vehicle detection framework operating without externally supplied inter-vehicle poses while retaining known intra-vehicle calibration. By unifying surface-guided query initialization with depth-guided iterative query refinement on top of a shared visual geometry backbone, \method{} couples multi-view visual geometry directly to 3D bounding box prediction. Under the reported OPV2V protocol, \method{} remains at 44.52\% scale-aligned AP$_{.5}$ across the evaluated pose-noise grid and crosses V2X-ViT between $k=2$ and $k=4$. Its normalized average inference cost is 0.55$\times$ the pseudo-point-cloud route in the recorded test-set benchmark. Fine-tuning on DAIR-V2X provides preliminary cross-domain pose evidence across extreme vehicle-roadside viewpoints; these results do not imply complete pose-error immunity or infrastructure-free deployment.
